HOW DOES OFFLINE DICE WORK?

This website uses virtual dice created with an algorithm that assigns equal probabilities to every possible outcome. By clicking on the 'Roll Again' button you can re-roll all the dice on this page.

This page allows you to choose any number of dice between 1 and 12, each having 6 sides and 6 possible colors:
Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue and Purple.

ABOUT US

Offline-Dice.com was created to provide the ultimate dice simulator without any annoying ads or interruptions. Enjoy a perfectly smooth rolling experience designed for live streamers, board games, or any other game of chance.

HOW DOES THE RANDOMIZATION WORK?

To guarantee 100% fair and completely unpredictable results, Offline Dice uses a Cryptographically Secure Pseudo-Random Number Generator (CSPRNG). This means our algorithm is mathematically proven to be completely random, giving every color the exact same probability of landing on every single roll.
